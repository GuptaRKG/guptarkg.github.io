Cycling

Sections of the national cross-country cycling network were completed and certified with the Bicycle Passport (available at any certification centre on the routes; see bike.go.kr).

  • Ara and Hangang bicycle paths (21 km + 192 km): along the Gyeong-In Ara waterway linking the West Sea to the Hangang River, then through Seoul past 24 bridges.
  • Bukhangang bicycle path (70 km): Seoul to Chuncheon via Uiamho Lake, following part of the disused Gyeongchun Railroad (1939–2010).
  • Gangwon East Coast path, 2023 (242 km): along the northern DMZ section and the Gangwon coastline, ending near Daejin-ri Beach, Goseong-gun.

Hiking

  • Bukhansan National Park: Baegundae (836.5 m), the highest peak, alongside Insubong (810.5 m) and Mangnyeongdae (799.5 m).
